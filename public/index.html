How the numbers are made

Every lab and your CV become sparse weighted vectors over one shared topic vocabulary. sim(A,B) = Σ wA·wB / (‖wA‖·‖wB‖) idf(t) = ln(1 + N / (1 + labs holding t)) Foundation = sim(you, lab) Leverage = Σ w·idf·(1 − w_lab) / Σ w·idf Growth = Σ w_lab·(1 − w_you) / Σ w_lab Direction = Σ w_lab·intent_t / Σ w_lab Growth alone is blind to intent: a lab with twenty topics you lack scores high even when none of them interest you. Direction is the correction. It is null, and its weight is dropped rather than imputed, when you have not said where you want to go. Transfer = sqrt(portable × targets) Your method and theory topics the lab lacks are portable; their system and application topics you lack are where they would go. A pivot needs both. Leverage is gated by min(1, Foundation/0.15): a lab you share nothing with cannot score well on what you would add to it. Focus = 1 − H(weights)/H_max Centrality = degree within the lab graph Shortlist = maximal marginal relevance, λ·fit − (1−λ)·redundancy
